# Demo Click Path

## Start URL

- Frontend: `http://127.0.0.1:3000`
- Backend: `http://127.0.0.1:8071`
- Demo Mode flag: `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_MODE=true`

## Page Sequence

1. Home
2. Campaign Effectiveness
3. Agent Performance Tracking
4. Policy Lapse Risk
5. Know Your Customer
6. Know Your Agent
7. AI Intelligence
8. Insight Evidence Hub

## Exact Click Path

1. Open Home and show the executive command center.
2. Click `Campaign Effectiveness`.
3. Highlight funnel, conversion, ROI, segment performance, recommendations, and data evidence.
4. Click `Agent Performance Tracking`.
5. Highlight total agents, premium generated, leaderboard, MAPA productivity, agent clusters, rising stars, MDRT-style producers, and coaching recommendations.
6. Click `Policy Lapse Risk`.
7. Highlight high-risk policies, premium at risk, lapse drivers, agent exposure, and retention actions.
8. Click `Know Your Customer`.
9. Show search, profile, policy portfolio, scores, timeline, recommendations, and evidence.
10. Click `Know Your Agent`.
11. Show search, profile, productivity, MAPA trend, customer portfolio, movement, risk, and actions.
12. Click `AI Intelligence`.
13. Select `Agency Manager`.
14. Ask `Which agents need coaching this month?`
15. Highlight validation-first execution path, answer summary, SQL, key data points, recommendations, and result preview.
16. Click `View Full Evidence`.
17. Highlight generated SQL, validation status, related tables, related columns, models, semantic context, lineage, diagnostics, and the Data, Models & Context Architecture section.

## Validated Questions For Demo Use

- Agency Manager: `Which agents need coaching this month?`
- Agency Manager: `Which agents have the highest premium at risk?`
- Campaign Manager: `Which campaign generated the highest policy conversion?`
- Campaign Manager: `Show campaign conversion rate by channel.`
- Executive Leadership: `What are the top risks to revenue this month?`
- Executive Leadership: `What are the top growth opportunities?`
- Data Analyst: `Show SQL for lapse risk by product.`
- Data Analyst: `Show campaign conversion rate by channel.`
- Insurance Agent: `Which customers should I contact first this week?`
- Insurance Agent: `Which customers are likely to lapse in the next 90 days?`

## Demo Safety Notes

- Do not use free-form questions in the client recording unless they have passed the readiness check.
- If a question is unsupported, show the clean unsupported message and move to a validated question.
- Avoid showing raw technical errors in the main business flow.
- Use Insight Evidence Hub for technical proof and limitations.
