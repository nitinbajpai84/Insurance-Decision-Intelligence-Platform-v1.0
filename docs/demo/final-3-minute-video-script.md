# Insurance Intelligence Product - 3 Minute Demo

## 1. Opening

Narration:
Welcome to the Insurance Intelligence Product. In three minutes, I will show how an insurer can improve growth, reduce lapse risk, improve campaign conversion, and help agency managers focus their teams using AI-backed decision intelligence.

Screen action:
Start on Home. Point to the executive KPIs and decision queue.

Data point:
New business premium, persistency, high lapse exposure, and campaign conversion.

Business value:
Leaders see revenue, risk, and action in one place.

## 2. Home

Narration:
The Home screen summarizes the most important decisions across customer, policy, agent, campaign, and model-score data.

Screen action:
Highlight Today's decision queue and the AI and Evidence Hub entry points.

Business value:
The platform turns analytics into prioritized actions.

## 3. Campaign Effectiveness

Narration:
Campaign managers can evaluate health upgrade campaign performance from targeting through policies issued. The view exposes where customers engage, where conversion breaks, and what next-best-follow-up should happen.

Screen action:
Open Campaign Effectiveness. Highlight funnel metrics, conversion analytics, segment performance, and recommendations.

Data point:
Targeted customers, opened, clicked, responded, leads, quotes, policies issued, premium generated, and ROI.

Business value:
Marketing can shift spend and follow-up toward segments most likely to convert.

## 4. Agent Performance Tracking

Narration:
Agency managers can compare agents by premium, persistency, conversion, MAPA activity, customer/product clusters, rising stars, and MDRT-style producers.

Screen action:
Open Agent Performance Tracking. Highlight leaderboard, MAPA productivity, peer clusters, risk alerts, and coaching recommendations.

Data point:
Premium generated, policies sold, average conversion, persistency, target achievement, and agents needing coaching.

Business value:
Managers can intervene early and scale successful agent behaviors.

## 5. Policy Lapse Risk

Narration:
The lapse risk view focuses retention effort on the policies and customers that matter most. It combines renewal timing, payment behavior, engagement, complaints, and model scores where available.

Screen action:
Open Policy Lapse Risk. Highlight high-risk policies, premium at risk, drivers, and retention actions.

Data point:
Premium at risk, high-risk policies, lapse drivers, products with increasing lapse, and agents associated with risk.

Business value:
The insurer protects revenue by prioritizing the right retention conversations.

## 6. AI Intelligence

Narration:
Now we move from dashboard insight to natural language intelligence. I will ask: Which agents need coaching this month?

Screen action:
Open AI Intelligence. Select Agency Manager. Generate the insight.

Data point:
Validated SQL status, execution status, row count, key data points, generated SQL, recommendations, and result preview.

Business value:
Business users get SQL-backed answers without writing SQL, and the platform blocks unsafe or unsupported output.

## 7. Insight Evidence Hub

Narration:
This is the proof layer. The Evidence Hub shows the question, role, intent, SQL, validation, execution, row count, result preview, tables, columns, model layer, semantic context, and data lineage.

Screen action:
Open View Full Evidence. Highlight SQL Evidence, Related Tables, Underlying Models, Semantic Context, Data Lineage, and Data, Models & Context Architecture.

Data point:
Real Supabase tables, schema-validated SQL, model scores, semantic documents, and result validation.

Business value:
The AI answer is auditable and explainable, not a black-box response.

## 8. Closing

Narration:
The result is one insurance intelligence layer for campaign growth, agency productivity, lapse prevention, and evidence-backed AI decisions.

Screen action:
Return to Home or finish on Evidence Hub.
